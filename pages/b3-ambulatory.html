B3 Ambulatory Assessment

Physical activity measured with movisens Move 3 activity sensors and momentary states reported in the movisensXS smartphone app in daily life.
Author

Maik Bieleke et al.

Published

20 November, 2026

Overview

Physical activity was measured with movisens Move 3 sensors worn at the hip. Psychological states were reported in the smartphone app movisensXS, in response to random and fixed prompts and before and during self-started exercise. In each test phase, data were collected for one measurement week (Monday to Sunday).

Test phases: p1 (pre), p2 (post), p3 (follow-up). See the study timeline.

Variable names

Most names are short abbreviations. Questionnaire variables follow the general naming rules. Building blocks in this project:

Building block Meaning Example
wt, sb, lpa, mpa, vpa, mvpa Wear time, sedentary behavior, light, moderate, vigorous, and moderate to vigorous physical activity mvpa
day (suffix) Total per day mvpaday
aff Momentary mood items aff01
affc, affa, affv Mood subscales: calmness, energetic arousal, valence affv
emo Exercise related emotions emo01
eff Effort during exercise effinst

The variable day counts the days since onboarding, and weekday gives the day of the week.
