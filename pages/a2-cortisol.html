A2 Cortisol

Salivary cortisol after awakening (cortisol awakening response) and a short morning questionnaire completed at each sampling point.
Author

Maik Bieleke et al.

Published

20 November, 2026

Overview

The cortisol data consist of saliva samples taken after awakening and a short questionnaire completed at each sampling point.

Test phases: p1 (pre), p2 (post), p3 (follow-up). See the study timeline.

Dataset Content One row is Key Rows Variables
